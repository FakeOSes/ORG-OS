Lemon Herb Chicken

Pan-seared chicken breasts with garlic, fresh herbs, and roasted vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless chicken breasts
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 cups green beans and baby potatoes

Method

  1. Season chicken with salt, pepper, oregano, and lemon zest.
  2. Sear in olive oil for 5–6 minutes per side until golden.
  3. Roast the potatoes and beans until tender, then toss with garlic and lemon juice.
  4. Serve chicken over the vegetables with extra herbs.

Chickpea Coconut Curry

A creamy, warming curry with chickpeas, spinach, and coconut milk.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 2 cups spinach

Method

  1. Cook onion in coconut oil until softened.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, and curry powder; cook 1 minute.
  3. Stir in chickpeas and coconut milk, then simmer for 12 minutes.
  4. Add spinach, season to taste, and serve with rice.

Mushroom Spinach Pasta

Garlicky pasta tossed with mushrooms, spinach, parmesan, and lemon.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 cups mushrooms, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 3 cups baby spinach
  • 1/2 cup parmesan
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Cook pasta until al dente and reserve a little pasta water.
  2. Brown mushrooms in butter, then add garlic and spinach.
  3. Toss the pasta with the vegetables, parmesan, and lemon juice.
  4. Add a splash of pasta water until glossy and creamy.

Berry Overnight Oats

Make-ahead oats with oats, yogurt, chia seeds, and fresh berries.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/3 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1/2 cup berries

Method

  1. Mix oats, milk, yogurt, chia, and honey in a jar.
  2. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
  3. Top with berries in the morning.
  4. Add nuts or granola for extra crunch.

Chocolate Chip Cookies

Classic chewy cookies loaded with dark chocolate and a little sea salt.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 1/4 cup flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Method

  1. Cream butter with both sugars until fluffy.
  2. Beat in eggs, then add flour and baking soda.
  3. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  4. Bake at 375°F for 9–11 minutes until edges are golden.

Sheet Pan Salmon

Roasted salmon with asparagus, potatoes, and a lemon-dill finish.

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 1 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 bunch asparagus
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 2 tbsp dill, chopped

Method

  1. Roast the potatoes at 400°F for 15 minutes.
  2. Add salmon and asparagus to the pan with olive oil and lemon slices.
  3. Roast until salmon is flaky and vegetables are tender.
  4. Finish with dill and serve immediately.
